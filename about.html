About Us

Who we are

A non-profit organisation striving to build an inclusive and socially cohesive society, a transformational education system, and innovative digital opportunities for vulnerable children and communities.

What we do

Local answers to a local history.

A quarter of a century of civil conflict in Sri Lanka had a huge influence on the country's socio-economic and environmental conditions. International aid was helpful, but imported answers were unable to fix core local problems on a long-term basis. So we were determined to make it possible for local people to tackle their own challenges — on their own terms.

Who we are

Education, innovation, enterprise.

We are a non-profit organisation that strives to build an inclusive and socially cohesive society, a transformational education system, and innovative digital initiatives — putting young people and vulnerable children in the driver's seat.

Mission

To achieve sustainable development goals through education, innovation, and entrepreneurship by empowering youths, promoting peace, design thinking, supporting economic ventures, improving rural regions, and safeguarding the environment.

Vision

To empower underserved communities through creative community development programs that promote self-sufficiency and enhance quality of life.

What holds us together

Five values. Five figures reaching for the same peace.

01

Equality

Every community we work with is treated as an equal partner, not a beneficiary.

02

Accountability

We report honestly on impact, spending, and setbacks alike.

03

Responsibility

We stay with a programme until the community can carry it forward alone.

04

Partnership

Government officials, religious dignitaries and local leaders, working as one.

05

Participation

Programmes are co-designed with the people they are meant to serve.

How we work

Three pillars of challenge-based learning.

Education

A transformational education system

We redesign how young people learn — replacing rote instruction with real, community-rooted problems that build critical thinking and design skills.

Innovation

Digital skills & design thinking

We bring practical computer literacy and human-centred design methods into rural settings, bridging the digital divide for vulnerable children.

Enterprise

Community empowerment & resilience

We help youth-led ventures move from idea to action, anchoring economic and social resilience directly inside the communities we serve.

Impact in the field

Our Work in Action

Engaging closely with local community officers, youth groups, and village leaders across Sri Lanka to drive meaningful change.

Community Leadership Circles

Collaborating directly with Grama Niladhari, Rural Development Societies (RDS) and Women's Rural Development Societies (WRDS).

Resettlement School Support

Delivering essential education materials, leadership camps, and digital learning opportunities for rural children.

Youth Empowerment & Dialogue

Fostering inter-community dialogue, creative problem solving, and entrepreneurship opportunities across Sri Lanka.

About Us

Who we are

A non-profit organisation striving to build an inclusive and socially cohesive society, a transformational education system, and innovative digital opportunities for vulnerable children and communities.

What we do

Local answers to a local history.

A quarter of a century of civil conflict in Sri Lanka had a huge influence on the country's socio-economic and environmental conditions. International aid was helpful, but imported answers were unable to fix core local problems on a long-term basis. So we were determined to make it possible for local people to tackle their own challenges — on their own terms.

Who we are

Education, innovation, enterprise.

We are a non-profit organisation that strives to build an inclusive and socially cohesive society, a transformational education system, and innovative digital initiatives — putting young people and vulnerable children in the driver's seat.

Mission

Sustainable development through education, innovation and entrepreneurship — empowering youth, promoting peace and digital skills, supporting economic ventures, improving rural regions, and safeguarding the environment.

Vision

To empower underserved communities through creative community development programmes that promote self-sufficiency and enhance quality of life.

Core Values

Five values, five figures, one peace sign.

01

Equality

Every community we work with is treated as an equal partner, not a beneficiary.

02

Accountability

We report honestly on impact, spending, and setbacks alike.

03

Responsibility

We stay with a programme until the community can carry it forward alone.

04

Partnership

Government officials, religious dignitaries and local leaders, working as one.

05

Participation

Programmes are co-designed with the people they are meant to serve.

How we work

Three pillars of challenge-based learning.

Education

A transformational education system

We redesign how young people learn — replacing rote instruction with real, community-rooted problems that build critical thinking and design skills.

Innovation

Digital skills & design thinking

We bring practical computer literacy and human-centred design methods into rural settings, bridging the digital divide for vulnerable children.

Enterprise

Community empowerment & resilience

We help youth-led ventures move from idea to action, anchoring economic and social resilience directly inside the communities we serve.

Field Engagement

Our work in action across Sri Lanka.

Working directly on the ground alongside village-level partners, educators, and community volunteers — creating real, lasting opportunities for children and youth.

Grassroots Leadership

Bringing together local leaders, international supporters, teachers, and coordinators to support community-driven programmes.

Resettlement School Support

Empowering children in rural and resettlement communities with confidence, education, and life-skills to shape their own future.

Youth Participation

Fostering peer collaboration, leadership skills, and inter-community trust among the next generation of changemakers.

Our Partners

Trusted links, at every level.

To increase access to services, we maintain close ties with government officials, religious dignitaries, and community leaders, as well as Rural Development Societies (RDS), Women's Rural Development Societies (WRDS) and youth-led organisations — building strong working relationships across legal, education and social services at the local, provincial and national level.

Government & Public Officials

Local, provincial and national government representatives across legal, education and social services.

RDS & WRDS

Rural Development Societies and Women's Rural Development Societies as grassroots delivery partners.

Youth-Led Organisations

Peer networks that extend our reach and put programme ownership in young hands.

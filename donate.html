Your Contribution Powers a Child's Future

Every rupee directly supports digital education, life-skills, and essential learning materials for children in rural resettlement communities across Sri Lanka.

How Your Donation Helps

LKR 2,500

Provides essential school stationery and learning supplies for 1 resettlement child.

LKR 5,000

Supports 1 month of digital literacy & typing training in our rural IT hub.

LKR 10,000

Funds creative life-skills and confidence-building workshops for a group of children.

LKR 25,000

Helps equip community computer centers with laptops, accessories and internet access.
